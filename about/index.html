My background

I got my start on a construction site

I was young when I took my first job as a construction laborer. Over time, I learned different trades and worked my way up to tradesman. When I was assigned to manage projects, I brought that on-site experience to planning the work, handling procurement, and reporting on progress.

Eventually, I started my own construction company. I began with small jobs and renovations and grew into taking on entire projects, from layout through finishing. I met with clients to plan the work, coordinated the crews, and tracked progress on site. If a plan changed, it was my job to make sure the clients and crews knew what came next.

That experience shapes how I work today. I like to understand what people need to get the job done, then make the plan clear enough for everyone to act on it.

I take the same approach to my independent web projects. I decide what each tool should do and how people will use it, then guide AI tools as they generate the code. I test the main workflows, review what needs to change, and keep building my own coding skills along the way.
