Independent project

How I built this portfolio

I wanted one place to show how my construction background connects to the work I do today. I chose what to feature, planned what each page should do, and guided the design and AI-assisted implementation.

Research & concept

Decide what each page needs to show

I researched design concepts and looked at how other sites use layout and motion to guide attention. I also reviewed my career notes and project samples to decide what each page should show.

Design & implementation

Turn the plan into pages

I used my own research and design workflows to compare ideas, set the visual direction, and decide where motion would help. I guided AI tools through the implementation, then reviewed the previews and directed revisions.

Testing & learning

Use the site as a visitor would

I reviewed desktop and mobile previews and used a walkthrough to check the CV downloads, project briefs, and practice tracker. That review helped me see where a touch interaction can get in the way of scrolling. The image divider now moves only when grabbed.
